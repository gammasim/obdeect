Ground-frame photons at grazing pointing (zenith above about 70°) were placed on an input plane above the incoming obscurers only. Where the dish reaches above that plane, photons started behind the mirror and were lost (about 20 % at zenith 86.8° for a dish reaching far above its camera). The plane now lies above the primary mirror (and the secondary if it blocks incoming light) too, and `trace_bunches` rejects a plane below such a mirror.
