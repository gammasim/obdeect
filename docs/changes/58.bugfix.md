`obdeect stream to-simtel` now refuses a camera- or telescope-frame stream whose efficiency status does not show the atmosphere as applied: a status without `atmosphere`, or with it `pending`. Before, a stream from `obdeect ray-tracing trace-stream` built on an unpropagated CORSIKA stream was converted without the atmosphere. The error names the factor and exits with status 1. Use `--allow-pending-atmosphere` (Python: `allow_pending_atmosphere=True`) where sim_telarray applies the atmosphere in that frame. Ground-frame streams and streams without any efficiency status are unchanged.
