Upward photons in the atmosphere stage (plane mode): sky-frame photons with cz > 0 are propagated from below the telescope plane (emitted between the observation level and the plane), bent by refraction (`bend_upward`, Snell's law, turning photons dropped as `turns_over`), and their extinction is the same integral as for downward photons. Frame G keeps a `cz` column when the input has one. The summary (Python and executable) gives `dropped_by_reason`, the dropped photons by reason; `dropped` is their total. Horizontal photons and photons emitted below the observation level are counted separately. `to-sky` converts upward G photons back to the sky frame.
