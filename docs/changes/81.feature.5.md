Ray tracing phase 2: non-sequential tracing. Every photon is traced after each reflection until it is detected, absorbed or escapes (at most 16 reflections, then the new status `bounce_limit`). Photons from below reflect on the secondary and then on the primary instead of being counted as `non_sequential`; the status is kept for stable numbering and no longer produced. A plane focal surface (no extent) hit from its receiving side before the last mirror is still `direct_focal`, a counted loss; an asphere focal surface is detected inside its outer radius. Sequential photons give the same results as before, bit for bit. The output gains `reflections`, the number of reflections of each photon.
