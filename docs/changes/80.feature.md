Photon stream format 1.1: the `cz` column may be positive (upward) or zero (horizontal) in every frame; without `cz` a photon moves downward. `obdeect stream to-simtel` rejects upward and horizontal photons in the ground and telescope frames explicitly, since sim_telarray cannot take them and does not store the sign of `cz`; camera-frame photons are unchanged.
