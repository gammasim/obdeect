Sphere runs (`ground_representation` `sphere`) take extinction, thinning and scattering. A sphere row is attenuated over its straight chord from the emission point to the row point, with the row point's altitude in place of the telescope altitude; a near-horizontal chord uses the chord integral of the extinction coefficient, which is stable as the cosine goes to zero. Scattered photons take the chord transmission when their path is near horizontal, and primaries with |cz| below 0.1 (including horizontal ones) are scattered along their straight path. Plane runs are unchanged.
