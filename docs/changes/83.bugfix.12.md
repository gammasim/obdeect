The photon-stream reader rejects invalid input that it used to accept: record nesting (photons outside a telescope record, `telescope_end` without `telescope`, nested `run` or `event`, and similar), non-finite column values, directions that are not unit vectors, upward photons in format 1.0 streams, and efficiency status records whose factors do not advance (pending, envelope, applied). After an error the reader stays failed, so later calls raise instead of returning records of a rejected stream. Writers are unchanged. Streams that obdeect writes itself are valid and read as before.
