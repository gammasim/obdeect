Sources presets use one azimuth convention: zenith and azimuth are astronomical (clockwise from north towards east). Sky sources (`muon`, `shower`, `night-sky`) give the direction the light comes from, so the track runs along the reverse; `muon` and `shower` had the azimuth sign and a 180 degree offset wrong. Beam devices (`laser-beam`, `lidar`, `beam-scan`) give the direction the beam points to, as before. The rule is in `docs/sources/presets.md` and the `--help` of each preset.
