Ray tracing accepts photons from below (upward, `cz > 0`): they start on a lower input plane under all optics, and their first hit is classified. New statuses `non_sequential` (the reflecting face of the secondary, out of sequence) and `direct_focal` (the focal surface from below) count as losses; full non-sequential tracing is planned. Horizontal photons (`cz = 0`) are rejected explicitly. `trace-stream`, `trace_bunches` and `star_photons` with `towards_source` below the telescope accept either sign of `cz`.
