Propagation refraction: the series of `bend` is used only where its truncation error is below 1e-7 m (`s_e |nu_obs - nu_e| <= 0.03 (1 - s_e)`, was `0.1 cos^2`); beyond it the quadrature bends the photon. At 87 degrees the stage error was 8.4e-3 m and 2.7e-2 ns (now 3.9e-4 m, float32 output, and 6.2e-6 ns). The group term of the default table is the bent-ray derivative (`nu / cos theta_ref`), as in the accurate-dispersion tables; the spherical positions change by at most 4e-5 m. Airmass near the horizon is computed in double. Scattering: the altitude of a given optical depth is solved in closed form (agrees with the former bisection to 5e-15 relative), which makes the scattering stage about 7 times faster with identical output.
