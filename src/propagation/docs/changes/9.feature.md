Geometry-only propagation: `dlufd-propagate --no-extinction` (Python: `extinction=False`) moves sky photons to the ground frame with refraction and air delay and assigns wavelengths, but applies no extinction, envelope or thinning. All photons keep their weight and the ledger records `atmosphere` as `pending`. It needs no transmission table and cannot be combined with `--envelope`, `--mode thinned`, `--scattering`, `--store-p` or the in-telescope path options.
